Interpretation guide

Compare subjective brain fog with objective accuracy, speed and memory across many sessions. Consider sleep, dose, anxiety, illness and events. Practice effects and natural fluctuations can mimic improvement. Dose and time may be too closely linked to distinguish causation.

About the measures

Symbol matching: 24 trials, accuracy and median response time on correct trials. Digit span: backward recall, two attempts per length. Word recall: 10 words presented for 30 seconds, followed by other tasks and free recall. Parallel word lists rotate, but are not psychometrically equated.

For more precise clinical assessment, use a clinician-administered standardised battery. Never change medication based on app results.
