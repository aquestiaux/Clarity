Cognitive testing

Complete tests at roughly the same time of day, on the same iPhone, without interruptions. Start with at least two familiarisation sessions; they are excluded from trend summaries.

This is a personal tracking tool, not a validated neuropsychological instrument. Small changes—especially reaction times—cannot be interpreted clinically. Alternate forms and repeated testing reduce but do not eliminate practice effects.

Tests: symbol matching (speed/attention), digit span backward (working memory), and delayed word recall (verbal memory). Scores are stored separately; there is no misleading single “IQ” score.

Interpretation guide

Compare subjective brain fog with objective accuracy, speed and memory across many sessions. Consider sleep, dose, anxiety, illness and events. Practice effects and natural fluctuations can mimic improvement. Dose and time may be too closely linked to distinguish causation.

Data & privacy

Entries are stored in this browser's local storage on this device. They are not uploaded by this app. Safari can delete site data, so export backups regularly. There is no passcode or encrypted storage in this prototype.

Restoring replaces existing entries after confirmation.

About the measures

Symbol matching: 24 trials, accuracy and median response time on correct trials. Digit span: backward recall, two attempts per length. Word recall: 10 words presented for 30 seconds, followed by other tasks and free recall. Parallel word lists rotate, but are not psychometrically equated.

For more precise clinical assessment, use a clinician-administered standardised battery. Never change medication based on app results.
